              Our story

              “Lalla Warda” was my grandmother.

              Every spring she distilled her roses in the courtyard, in a copper qettara. The neighbours called her Lalla Warda, Lady Rose. I grew up among her bottles of rose water and the ghassoul she prepared for the Friday hammam.

              Trained as a chemist, I took up her craft with traceable ingredients, short formulas and laboratory testing. Everything is still filled by hand in Kenitra, in small batches.

              Kenza Amrani, founder
